How it works

01

Ratings

Every finished match updates team Elo, a map-by-map Elo, and a rating for each player that moves with him when he changes teams. Recent HLTV stats (rating, ADR, KAST, swing) are tracked per player.

02

Model

A logistic regression and a gradient-boosted tree model read about 35 features: rating gaps, the announced lineups, roster continuity, map pools, form, rest, head-to-head, LAN, and HLTV and VRS rank. Their blend is calibrated on the most recent matches.

03

Getting stronger

New results reach the ratings daily and the model refits on everything so far. Weekly challenger tests tune the settings. Every published number is logged before the match and graded after, so the live record is real.
